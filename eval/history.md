# What the November 2026 study measured

The first study compared pi's default, a paper-derived recap, and a brief with copied user messages. It considered first compactions and chains, where each strategy consumed its own previous summaries. It did not establish an overall winner by the rules set in advance.

The follow-up tested the shipped brief (D) against the two experimental alternatives (B/C), not pi's default. D used a 15,000-character copied-history cap, shorter instructions, and the recent messages pi retained. On 30 fresh first-compaction points, two model judges read the source and ranked three anonymized checkpoints. Opus 5.5 ranked D first on 30/30; GPT-6 Astra on 25/30. On ten reordered examples both kept their top choices. Cases were sampled by conversation lineage; development cases were kept separate. No agent resumed those tasks as part of the comparison.

These are aggregate results from private sessions. Neither transcripts, generated checkpoints, call caches nor the verbatim paper prompt are redistributed. The public runner is **not a reproduction of those 30 results**. It uses two candidates, explicit rubrics and a constrained resume task; its fictional examples demonstrate the procedure rather than estimating real-world task success. Background: [Belcak et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05306).
